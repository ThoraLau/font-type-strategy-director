# Roles and work products

The Skill combines three professional perspectives. They overlap, but each protects against a different failure.

## Creative Type Director

### Purpose

Translate business and brand intent into a coherent typographic voice, direct the work, align stakeholders, and ensure that the result can be produced and governed.

### Work blocks

- discovery: brand history, audience, category, competitors, markets, languages, touchpoints, and constraints;
- voice definition: verbal/visual attributes, productive tensions, heritage versus future, distinctiveness thresholds;
- direction: reference territories, custom-versus-library decision, family architecture, variable-font opportunity;
- collaboration: brand strategists, graphic designers, type designers, engineers, native-script experts, legal/procurement, and client leadership;
- evaluation: creative reviews, application prototypes, language reviews, legibility and production tests;
- communication: rationale, client presentations, workshops, decision records, and executive alignment;
- governance: licensing, distribution, font management, templates, training, rollout, and consistency.

### Deliverables

- type strategy brief;
- 3-5 creative territories;
- selection or custom-type recommendation;
- prototype applications;
- decision rationale and risk register;
- implementation and governance plan.

### Failure prevented

A beautiful font that cannot express the brand consistently, win stakeholder support, scale globally, or survive implementation.

Reference signals: Monotype describes Creative Type Directors as senior design experts, client advisers, presenters, collaborators with sales/marketing, and leaders of custom brand-font work.

## Brand Typography Designer

### Purpose

Turn the strategic voice into a usable typographic system across real communications.

### Work blocks

- selection: inspect families, styles, construction, spacing, characters, numerals, language coverage, and licenses;
- role design: assign display, headline, body, UI, data, caption, quote, and utility responsibilities;
- hierarchy: sizes, weights, widths, line lengths, leading, tracking, optical sizes, casing, and emphasis;
- pairing: decide whether contrast is required and ensure each family has a distinct job;
- application: advertising, packaging, editorial, ecommerce, social, motion, environmental, product, and internal documents;
- accessibility: realistic reading sizes, zoom/reflow, dense UI, contrast, dyslexia claims avoidance, and fallback behavior;
- specification: tokens, CSS/app configuration, templates, do/don't examples, and exception rules;
- QA: browser/OS rendering, font loading, missing glyphs, substitution, print output, and asset handoff.

### Deliverables

- font shortlist and comparison specimens;
- role matrix and hierarchy;
- typographic tokens;
- application prototypes;
- brand typography guidelines;
- production package and QA checklist.

### Failure prevented

A strategically appropriate typeface used inconsistently, illegibly, or without clear roles.

## Typography Researcher

### Purpose

Build the historical, cultural, formal, and audience evidence that makes the strategy more than taste.

### Work blocks

- archive research: specimens, lettering manuals, foundry records, period publications, signage, packaging, and previous brand materials;
- formal analysis: construction, proportions, contrast, terminals, rhythm, text color, and family behavior;
- genealogy: distinguish source, revival, synthesis, adaptation, and later cultural reuse;
- semiotic analysis: document associations, counter-readings, category conventions, subcultural meanings, and cliché risks;
- use research: study the face in real editorial, digital, spatial, and commercial contexts;
- audience research: interviews, sorting tasks, association tests, contextual mockups, preference/rejection language, and segment disagreement;
- multiscript research: local conventions, native expertise, parallel texture, script-specific hierarchy, and cultural review;
- evidence management: provenance, dates, quotations, uncertainty, conflicting readings, and source quality.

### Deliverables

- annotated reference board;
- historical/context timeline;
- semiotic signal map;
- audience association findings;
- evidence ledger with fact/inference labels;
- cultural and multiscript risk notes.

### Failure prevented

An attractive but historically false, culturally shallow, stereotyped, or unsupported narrative.

## Combined operating model

```text
Typography Researcher -> evidence, lineages, audience readings, risks
          |                                      |
          v                                      v
Creative Type Director -> strategy, territories, decisions, alignment
          |
          v
Brand Typography Designer -> roles, hierarchy, prototypes, production
          |
          v
Research + direction review -> test, revise, document, govern
```

One person may perform all three roles, but the outputs should remain distinguishable. Do not let creative direction invent research evidence, or let a scoring tool make the final design judgment.
